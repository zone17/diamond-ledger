The trade every scorekeeper makes

Every app wants your thumbs on the screen for every pitch — so you score the game instead of watching it. And when it's done, the data is trapped: no app exports the Retrosheet format the rest of baseball actually uses.

  • Tap, tap, tap. Per-pitch screen interaction pulls your eyes off the field.
  • Not really official. Volunteer-kept stats get discounted; there's no clean export.
  • Locked in. The dominant app has no public API and no Retrosheet event-file export.

Diamond Ledger removes the screen from the moment

Watch the play. Say what you saw — "ground ball to short, threw him out at first." A deterministic scoring engine applies the official rules, renders standard notation, and exports a Retrosheet-compatible file. Judgment calls (hit vs. error, earned vs. unearned) stop and ask you — one tap.

Who this is for first. Travel, high-school, and college scorers and statisticians, official scorers, and Retrosheet/SABR archivists — the people who need the book to be right and exportable. (Parents and casual fans: you're next — join the list.)
