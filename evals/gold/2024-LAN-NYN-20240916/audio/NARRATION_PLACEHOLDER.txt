NARRATION PROTOCOL (step H-1)
------------------------------
Game: LAN (Dodgers) vs NYN (Mets), 2024-09-16, Dodger Stadium
Retrosheet game ID: LAN2024091601

OPTION A — Record audio narration (preferred):
  1. Download or obtain the play-by-play from a public source
     (Baseball Reference: https://www.baseball-reference.com/boxes/LAN/LAN202409160.shtml
      or Retrosheet: https://www.retrosheet.org/game.htm → 2024 season → LAN home games).
  2. For each plate appearance, speak naturally as if you were a scorer describing the play,
     e.g.: "Ground ball to shortstop, threw him out at first."
     Each utterance should be 2–10 seconds, representing a SINGLE plate appearance.
  3. Record a single audio file covering all plays in order.
     Preferred format: .m4a (AAC), mono, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps.
  4. Save as: audio/narration.m4a
  5. Optionally record one file per play: audio/play-001.m4a, audio/play-002.m4a, etc.
     (plays numbered 1-based, top to bottom across all innings)

OPTION B — Verbatim transcript (if audio unavailable):
  1. Produce a text transcript in the format:
       Inning <N> <top|bottom>, play <seq>: <narration>
     Example:
       Inning 1 top, play 1: Ground ball to shortstop, threw him out at first.
       Inning 1 top, play 2: Pop fly to center, caught for the out.
  2. Each line is one plate appearance. Judgment plays should be described as ambiguous
     (e.g., "Ground ball to shortstop, ball glanced off his glove, batter reached first —
      could be a hit or an error").
  3. Save as: audio/narration.txt

IMPORTANT: The narration must be produced BEFORE or INDEPENDENTLY of the Reisner hand-scoring
(step H-2). The scorer in H-2 should ideally be scoring from this audio, not from the EVN.
This independence is what makes H3 field accuracy rather than self-consistency.

DELETE THIS FILE once narration.m4a or narration.txt is present.
